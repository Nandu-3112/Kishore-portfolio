01 / ORGANIZATIONAL BUILDSTools I built for my organization.

I built these three for the team I work in, to fix problems we hit every day. The demos work; the names, numbers and links are made up.

Demo · dummy dataReverse pyramid

Whom do I report to? The hierarchy, flipped.

Most org charts start at the CEO and fan out. When a deal is stuck, a consultant needs the opposite: their own name first, then each person above them. This chart puts the team on top and the founder at the point of the pyramid.

What went into it
Each person's "reports to" field from the HR system. Click a name and their full reporting line lights up, level by level. The same chain feeds the contact ladders in the Programs Hub.

Programs Hub: the right link and the right person, every time.

Every program has its own brochures, sheets and FAQs, and its own people to ask. They used to be scattered across chats. Now each program has one page with its links and a contact ladder: start with the person at the bottom and escalate upward.

What went into it
Sign-in limited to company email, an admin panel for links, and escalation ladders built automatically from the reporting structure.

Global Sales Desk: when to call, and what to quote.

International leads go cold when you call at the wrong hour or quote in the wrong currency. One screen now shows who's in their best calling window, when that window falls in India time, and the program price in their local money.

What went into it
40 time zones from the US to New Zealand, with daylight saving handled automatically. It knows Gulf Friday–Saturday weekends, Friday prayer hours and India's 9 AM–9 PM calling rule. Prices are rounded to a figure you can say on a call.
Where it stands
I use it on my own calls, and I wrote an 81-test-case spec so engineering can build it into our internal tools.
